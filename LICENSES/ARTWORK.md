# Carousel artwork licenses

kUI's card image ships per-platform carousel art: background panels at
`.system/res/carousel/` (with style variants under
`.system/themes/artbook/`) and platform logos at
`.system/res/carousel/logos/`.

The panels and logos come from the **Art Book Next** theme by Anthony
Caccese (github.com/anthonycaccese/art-book-next-es), licensed
**CC BY-NC-SA** — attribution, non-commercial use, share-alike. kUI is
free software and ships this art unmodified apart from resizing; the
license text and full credits live in the upstream repository.
